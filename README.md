# PGX25 (beta)

PGX25 is a Mac app for producers: every version and stem of a song in one place, finished
songs and albums, visualizer videos and post planning for your socials.

This page only holds the beta downloads.

## Install

1. Download **PGX25.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/avskixxx/PGX25-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open the zip and drag **PGX25** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the developer (the beta isn't notarized yet):
   - Click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
   - Scroll down to "PGX25 was blocked" and click **Open Anyway**, then **Open**.
   - You only do this once.

After that, PGX25 updates itself: when a new version is out, it asks, you click
**Install Update**, and it relaunches. Your songs and settings stay put.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Everything stays on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.
