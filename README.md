# pgx8 (beta)

pgx8 is a Mac app for producers: every version and stem of a song in one place, finished
songs and albums, visualizer videos and post planning for your socials.

## The beta is invite-only

Testers get the download link directly. There's nothing to download from this page right now.

pgx8 needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Everything stays on your Mac;
nothing is uploaded.

## What this page holds

pgx8's update feed and the stem-splitter models the app downloads when you first split a song.
